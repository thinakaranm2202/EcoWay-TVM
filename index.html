Engineering & Architecture

System Architecture & Methodology

Designed as a production-grade IoT & AI Smart City prototype for Tiruvannamalai Municipality and Tamil Nadu Environmental Protection Board.

Edge IoT Nodes
Layer 1 • Hardware Sensing

Solar-powered ESP32 nodes equipped with PMS7003 optical laser dust sensors, MQ-135 gas analyzers, and DHT22 thermo-hygrometers stationed at key transit intersections.

LoRaWAN Gateway
Layer 2 • Long-Range RF

Ultra-low-power SX1276 telemetry mesh covering the entire 14 km Girivalam ring with 868 MHz frequency band and 99.8% packet reliability even during power grid outages.

AI Predictive Core
Layer 3 • Deep Learning

Bidirectional LSTM neural network forecasting 24–48 hour AQI trajectories, particulate accumulation hotspots, and early-warning waste-burning anomalies.

Civic Command Center
Layer 4 • Web Platform

Responsive real-time civic portal with GIS heat mapping, automated signal timing recommendations, and municipal squad rapid dispatch triggers.
